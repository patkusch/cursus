Expense claims

An employee fills in the claim form and attaches the receipts. The employee
then submits the claim to their line manager.

The line manager checks the claim against the travel policy. If the claim is
within policy, the line manager approves it. If it is not, the manager sends
the claim back to the employee with a note, and the employee corrects the claim
and submits it again.

Approved claims go to the finance team. Finance checks whether the claim is
over 500 pounds. Claims over 500 pounds need sign-off from the finance
director. Once a claim is cleared, finance schedules the payment for the next
pay run and the system emails a confirmation to the employee.

The finance director reviews large claims within five working days.
