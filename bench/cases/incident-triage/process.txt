The service desk logs the incident and then rates its severity. The service desk
adds a low severity incident to the weekly backlog. The service desk assigns a
medium severity incident to the duty engineer. The service desk escalates a high
severity incident to the incident manager. The incident manager opens a call
with the affected teams and then assigns the incident to the duty engineer. The
duty engineer fixes the incident. The service desk closes the ticket once the
incident is fixed.
