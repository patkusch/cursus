A requester raises a purchase request. Procurement checks whether the supplier
is on the approved list. If the supplier is not on the list, procurement rejects
the request. If the supplier is on the list, procurement checks the amount. For
requests under 1,000 pounds, procurement places the order straight away.
Requests of 1,000 pounds or more go to the budget holder. If the budget holder
approves the request, procurement places the order. If the budget holder
refuses, procurement rejects the request.
